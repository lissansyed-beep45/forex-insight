Technical analysis checklist

Use these checks alongside the chart. This free version does not automatically read the chart or create guaranteed signals.

TrendEMA 20 / 50
MomentumRSI 14
ConfirmationMACD
  • Trend: Is price above or below EMA 20 and EMA 50? Are the averages sloping clearly?
  • RSI: Above 50 can indicate stronger upward momentum; below 50 can indicate stronger downward momentum. Above 70 or below 30 can mean momentum is stretched, not an automatic trade.
  • MACD: Check whether the MACD line is above or below its signal line and whether that agrees with the trend.
  • Levels: Mark recent swing highs/lows and wait for a candle close before judging a breakout.
  • Risk: Decide in advance where the idea is invalidated. Avoid trading money you cannot afford to lose.
